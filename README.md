## About

I build production AI products, teach machine learning as an adjunct professor, and research the safety of the multi-agent systems I work with every day. The thread across all of it is deploying capable AI responsibly.

Day to day I design and ship systems built on teams of LLM agents — role hierarchies, dispatch patterns, and shared-memory coordination. Working with these systems made their safety properties concrete rather than abstract: the coordination that makes an agent team effective also makes its behavior harder to oversee. My research applies that hands-on experience to the **AI control** problem for multi-agent deployments.

## Research

**Fragmented Oversight: How Multi-Agent Team Structure Affects Monitorability Under Distributed Attacks** — *in progress, 2026*
An AI-control study of how coordinated agents built on the same model can pursue a hidden objective, and how team topology and monitor placement change whether that behavior stays detectable. Built on Inspect and ControlArena; all adversarial testing runs inside a synthetic sandbox against self-built monitors.

## Building

**AI study platform** — *co-founder & lead developer.* A guidance system that models what a student has and hasn't mastered from their own course material and generates targeted practice from it, so the value is in the feedback loop rather than the generation.

**Autonomous laboratory robotics** — *co-founder & lead developer.* Control software that plans experiments and drives lab instruments and robotics, with a deterministic safety layer kept independent of the AI: no model output reaches a physical action without passing validators that enforce the system's hard limits.

I'm the lead developer on both, and I build and orchestrate my own teams of AI agents to develop and review the code — the same hands-on multi-agent engineering that grounds my research.
