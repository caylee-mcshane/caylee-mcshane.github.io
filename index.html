Data scientist · applied ML & multi-agent systems

I build with teams of AI agents, and I study how to keep them safe when they work together.

My day-to-day is designing and shipping multi-agent systems. My research turns that same experience toward safety: how coordinated agents can slip past oversight, and which team structures and monitoring strategies keep them accountable.

About

I'm a data scientist with 6 years of experience tuning and deploying machine learning and LLM systems. Recently my work has centered on orchestrating teams of agents — role hierarchies, dispatch patterns, and shared-memory coordination — to get complex tasks done reliably.

Working with these systems every day made their safety properties concrete rather than abstract: the same coordination that makes a team effective also makes its behavior harder to oversee. That's what pulled me toward AI safety, and specifically toward the control problem for multi-agent deployments.

Research

In progress · 2026

Fragmented Oversight: How Multi-Agent Team Structure Affects Monitorability Under Distributed Attacks

An AI-control study of what happens when several agents built on the same model coordinate toward a hidden objective. It measures how team topology and monitor placement change whether coordinated misbehavior stays detectable, and which monitoring strategies best restore oversight. Built on Inspect and ControlArena; attacks run only inside a synthetic sandbox against self-built monitors.
